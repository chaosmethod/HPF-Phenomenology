# Chronological Governor

## 1. Executive Summary
In the Holographic Projection Framework (HPF), the "Arrow of Time" is not a fundamental asymmetry of the substrate, but an emergent execution bias. While the Quantum Partitioned Reversible Cellular Automaton (QPRCA) is strictly bijective and reversible (Axiom II), macroscopic irreversibility arises from Update Availability ($\alpha$) and the computational cost of "un-scrambling" high-entropy data. The Arrow of Time is the path of least computational resistance for the Scheduler to maintain Topological Stability.

## 2. Proper Time as Regulated Throughput
Time is not a primitive parameter but a measure of completed updates.
* **Scheduler Time ($t_{sched}$):** The global, uniform, and non-dilating execution clock of the substrate.
* **Proper Time ($\tau$):** The locally experienced duration derived from the frequency of successful coherent updates.
* **The Relation:** $$\frac{d\tau(x)}{dt_{sched}} \propto \sqrt{\alpha(x)}$$

## 3. The Mechanism of One-Way Execution
The "Governor" that prevents time-reversal is rooted in Entropic Saturation and Bandwidth Truncation.

### The Path of Entropic Expansion
* **Axiom IV (Topological Stability):** The vacuum statistically saturates available channel capacity.
* **Forward Bias:** Degrees of freedom that can exist will be excited to fill the bandwidth (the "Goldilocks" solution).
* **Reversal Penalty:** Reversing the projection requires the Scheduler to selectively reduce entropy, which contradicts the maximal-entropy constrained state of the vacuum.

### The Decoherence Gate
* **Phase Transition:** As Entropic Flux ($S_f$) increases from the Einstein State ($S_f < 1.4$) toward the Decoherence State ($S_f > 5.79$), geometry collapses into information.
* **Complexity Lock:** Once data is redistributed via the Reversible Redistribution Map ($R_i$), the "error correction" required to reconstruct the previous laminar state exceeds the local Update Capacity ($\alpha$).

## 4. Synchronization via Lattice Mirror Symmetry
The Arrow of Time is stabilized by the requirement that the Direct View and the Reflected View remain in phase.
* **The Mirror Lock:** Because an entangled system is a mirror image on the lattice, the Scheduler must enforce Unitarity Health ($U_{health}$) to prevent the two projections from drifting apart.
* **Chronological Stabilization:** Reversing one side of the mirror without the other would cause an immediate Authority Collapse in the MDEA-HPF architecture.

## 5. Falsifiability
* **Stochastic Jitter:** The "Arrow" should exhibit a microscopic noise floor proportional to $\sqrt{N} L_{vac}$ as the lattice updates grainily.
* **Entropy Scaling:** In high-congestion regimes ($\sigma \to 1$), the "Arrow" should experience extreme dilation (freezing) rather than reversal, confirming that time is a function of throughput.
